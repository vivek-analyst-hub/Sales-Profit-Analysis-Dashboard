📊 Sales & Profit Performance Analysis Dashboard
Hey! 👋 Welcome to my Sales Analysis data analytics project.

In this project, I explored business sales data to track revenue growth, product rankings, regional performance, and payment trends. I handled the complete end-to-end workflow—starting with data cleaning and removing duplicates in Excel, and finally designing an interactive, professional dashboard in Power BI.

🛠️ Tools & Tech Stack
Microsoft Excel: For data cleaning, checking duplicate rows, and prepping the raw data structures.

Power BI: For building the interactive dashboard, writing custom DAX measures, and setting up clean visual layouts.

📈 Key Metrics Tracked
Total Sales: 4.51M

Total Profit: 1.66M

Profit Margin: 36.87%

Total Orders: 1,500

Average Order Value: 3,007.08

📑 Dashboard Visuals & Structure
To present the insights clearly, I organized the dashboard into key analytical components:

Sales & Profit Trend (Line Chart): Tracks how sales and profit moved month by month over time.

Sales by Region (Bar Chart & Table): Highlights regional performance and revenue contributions across Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, and South America.

Sales by Product Ranking (Bar Chart): Ranks products (Printer, Tablet, Laptop, Desk, Monitor, Chair, Phone) based on overall sales.

Sales Share by Payment Method (Donut Chart): Shows how customers prefer paying through Online, Credit Card, Cash, Gift Card, and Debit Card.

Salesperson Summary Table: Tracks individual team member performance including sales, profit, and profit margins.

🎨 Dashboard Design & Theme
I designed this dashboard with a clean corporate look, using structured KPI cards, a dark-themed sidebar filter panel, and a consistent color palette for a smooth user experience.
